# Scoring

Total = Problem Severity (25) + Commercial Value (20) + Ability to Pay (15) + Solution Fit (15) + Accessibility (10) + Evidence Quality (10) + Urgency (5).

## Priority bands
- A: 90–100
- B: 80–89
- C: 70–79
- WATCH: 60–69
- REJECT: <60

## Anti-inflation rules
1. Score only what evidence supports.
2. Unknown is not a zero-quality excuse; it is an uncertainty that should reduce confidence/accessibility/ability-to-pay where appropriate.
3. Never infer exact revenue from appearance.
4. A visible problem with low commercial consequence should not score highly.
5. A high-ticket niche does not automatically mean high ability to pay.
6. Accessibility means a plausible route to a relevant decision-maker, not merely a public phone number.

## Expected-value framework
Use qualitatively:
`Expected Opportunity = Problem Severity × Solution Fit × Ability to Pay × Accessibility × Evidence Confidence`

Do not turn this into fake currency forecasts. Use it to rank competing opportunities.

## Re-score triggers
Re-score when material new evidence appears, decision-maker/contact changes, a new competitor comparison changes severity, the business launches a new campaign/product, or outreach/meeting results provide market evidence.
