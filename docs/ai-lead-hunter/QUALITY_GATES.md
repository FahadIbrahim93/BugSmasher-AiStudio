# Quality Gates

## Gate A — candidate
- Real, active business
- Plausible service/product
- Public evidence
- No obvious duplicate

## Gate B — qualified
- Concrete observable problem
- Commercial consequence explained
- Solution fit identified
- Ability to pay assessed without fabricated financial claims
- Contact path assessed
- Score defensible

## Gate C — outreach-ready
- At least one strong source for every material claim
- Personalization hook is genuinely specific
- Offer matches the strongest problem
- Contact is public and verified where claimed
- No unsupported promise
- No fabricated urgency

## Gate D — send authorization
- Human explicitly approved
- Correct recipient/channel
- Message version recorded
- Opt-out/do-not-contact check passed

## Gate E — outcome
- Actual response/event recorded
- Status transition justified
- Next action defined
- Learning captured

A lead that fails a gate must not be promoted merely to increase output volume.
