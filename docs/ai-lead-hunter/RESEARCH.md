# Research & Evidence Protocol

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Official website/page
2. Official social profile
3. Public government/business registry or authoritative directory
4. Public business listing/reviews
5. Reputable third-party publication
6. Search snippets only as discovery aids, not sole support for consequential claims

## Claim format
`CLAIM` → `CLASSIFICATION` → `SOURCE` → `CAPTURE TIME` → `CONFIDENCE`.

## Observation rules
Record exact page/context where practical. Distinguish a page being poorly designed from a measurable technical defect. A technical metric requires an actual measurement. A missing feature is not automatically a business problem unless its commercial relevance can be explained.

## Decision-maker rules
Only mark a person as decision-maker when a reliable public source identifies their role. If role is uncertain, store the person as a public contact with `UNVERIFIED` role.

## Contact rules
Public business email/phone/contact forms are allowed as business contact data. Do not infer private addresses. Do not collect unnecessary personal information.

## Competitor research
Use competitors to establish relative opportunity, not to claim that the target is objectively inferior without comparable evidence.

## Freshness
Re-verify time-sensitive contact information and active status before outreach. Historical evidence remains historical; append newer evidence rather than rewriting the old record.
